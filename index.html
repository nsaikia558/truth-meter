Camera + mic stress test

Answers a list of questions while the camera and microphone track heart rate, eyes, face tension and voice timing. Your truthful baseline answers set your normal level. Each test answer is marked Truth if it stays below that level and Lie if it rises above it, with a confidence score.

Experimental, not a real lie detector. It measures stress and effort, which nerves, lighting and movement also change. Use it only with people who agree to it, and never to make real decisions about anyone.

Video and audio are processed in this browser only. Nothing is recorded or uploaded.
