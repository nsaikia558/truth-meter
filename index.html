Camera stress test

First the person answers some practice questions: some truthfully, some with a lie on purpose. That teaches the app how this person looks when telling the truth and when lying. Then each test question is compared with both.

Experimental, not a real lie detector. It mostly measures how fast and how tense someone answers. Nerves, lighting and movement change that too. Use it only with people who agree to it, and never to make real decisions about anyone.

Video and audio are processed in this browser only. Nothing is recorded or uploaded.
